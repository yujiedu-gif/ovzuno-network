An NFT, or non-fungible token, is a distinct blockchain token record. It can be associated with digital content, but the token is not necessarily the content itself. Holding or controlling a token does not, on its own, establish ownership of a linked file, copyright, or other rights; those claims require separate supporting information and applicable terms.

## NFT Meaning and How It Works

“Non-fungible” means distinct rather than interchangeable on a one-for-one basis. In plain language, an NFT is a particular token recognized under the rules of a blockchain system. It is not simply an image, video, or other digital file, even when an NFT is presented alongside one.

The token, the content associated with it, and any rights connected with that content are separate things. They may be linked by technical records or terms, but the existence of a link does not make them identical. This distinction is central to understanding what an NFT means and what it can establish.

## What the blockchain records

A blockchain record can establish information that the relevant network and token system recognize about a token. The details vary across systems, so a description of one token standard should not be treated as a universal rule for all NFTs. The record also does not independently verify every claim about the item a token is said to represent.

Control of a blockchain address is another distinct concept. For an Ethereum externally owned account, the corresponding private key controls the account. A valid signature can support attribution of a transaction to control of that key, but it does not by itself identify the human, organization, device, or service controlling it. This helps distinguish a technical statement about key control from a claim about a person’s identity or legal rights.

Accordingly, a token record can be evidence about what a blockchain system recognizes, while claims about authorship, authority, the linked item, or legal entitlement need additional evidence. A record alone cannot settle every question about how a token relates to an off-chain work or asset.

## How an NFT connects to digital content

A token may be linked to content through associated information or a reference. The content might be an image or another digital item, but the reference and the file it points to are not the same thing. Whether the content is stored with the token or obtained separately depends on the particular system and its implementation.

That distinction matters when asking whether content will remain accessible or whether it matches a description. The token record alone does not establish the availability, integrity, or accuracy of separate content. Those questions depend on the relevant storage arrangements and the information used to connect the token to that content. A general NFT definition cannot establish how a specific item is stored or whether its associated material will remain available.

A careful description therefore avoids saying simply that an NFT “is” the artwork or file. More precisely, it is a token record that may be associated with content. Identifying the content and understanding how the association works requires looking beyond the bare idea of a token.

## What holding an NFT does—and does not—mean

Holding or controlling a token refers to the token under the blockchain system’s rules. It does not automatically establish ownership of a linked digital file, copyright in an underlying work, or permission to use that work in a particular way. The blockchain record and the rights or permissions associated with content should be interpreted separately.

A rights or license reference may be associated with a token, but a technical standard or token label does not by itself determine whether a right is legally enforceable or how broad it is. The relevant documentation and applicable terms matter. A token record can be part of the information considered, but it is not a substitute for evidence of what rights were granted or who had authority to grant them.

This is a general technical explanation, not a legal conclusion about a particular NFT or jurisdiction. A specific ownership or license claim requires examining the applicable documentation and circumstances. The narrow, reliable distinction is between what the blockchain system records about a token and what separate evidence establishes about content or rights.

## Questions an NFT definition cannot settle

A general definition cannot establish whether a particular platform supports a token, what access conditions apply, or what fees may be involved. Those details need current, platform- and network-specific information. Similarly, security, custody, and recovery questions depend on the relevant wallet, contract, and procedures; they cannot be answered from the word “NFT” alone.

Tax, regulation, and compliance questions also fall outside a technical definition and require current information for the relevant jurisdiction and circumstances. Keeping these limits clear gives a useful working meaning: an NFT is a distinct blockchain token record that may be associated with digital content, while the content and any rights connected to it require separate identification and evidence.
