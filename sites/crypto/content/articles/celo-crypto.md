## What does Celo crypto refer to?

A blockchain network is infrastructure for recording transactions and other data. A cryptocurrency, in a technical definition, is a digital asset, credit, or unit that can be cryptographically transferred between users of a blockchain network. The network and an asset used on it are related, but they are not interchangeable concepts.

More broadly, a digital token can be a digital ledger record about an asset and its owner, with programmable rules governing use or transfer. That description is general: a ledger record alone does not determine legal title, enforceability, or the quality of the asset it represents. It also does not identify the specific assets or functions of any particular network.

For Celo, confirm the network’s current name and purpose and the identity and role of its native crypto asset in current Celo documentation. Avoid inferring those details from a wallet display, an exchange listing, or a product’s marketing label. A wallet is an application or tool through which a user may interact with digital assets; it is not itself the blockchain network or the asset. Likewise, a payment service is a separate offering unless its documentation establishes otherwise.

## How the Celo network relates to digital-asset payments

Cryptocurrency networks can support digital peer-to-peer exchange using distributed record-keeping rather than a conventional central transaction processor. A transfer recorded on a network is also not the same thing as every step in a retail payment.
