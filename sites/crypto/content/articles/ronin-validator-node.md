## What to verify before operating or delegating

Use this checklist to decide whether the official documentation answers the practical questions relevant to your intended participation:

1. **Role and duties:** Find the current definition of a Ronin validator and the actions it must perform. Identify which responsibilities belong to the protocol, the operator, and any separate service or application.
2. **Eligibility and participation:** Verify the available pathways, selection rules, required commitments, continuing conditions, and any restrictions. Confirm whether governance or protocol updates can change them.
3. **Technical setup:** Check supported software and versions, hardware, connectivity, ports or network settings, synchronization expectations, and upgrade instructions. Use only specifications explicitly published for Ronin.
4. **Monitoring and continuity:** Find the documented methods for tracking validator status, detecting missed duties or outages, and handling maintenance or incidents. Confirm whether the network specifies any response expectations.
5. **Keys and recovery:** Identify required keys and their permissions, the approved security and backup guidance, and how to recover or rotate access after a suspected compromise or equipment failure.
6. **Participation terms:** Verify current fees, staking requirements, reward rules, penalties, withdrawal or exit conditions, and any relevant change notices. If a term is not documented, mark it unresolved.
7. **Personal obligations:** Confirm applicable legal, regulatory, tax, and compliance obligations for your own circumstances with qualified sources. The available evidence supports no jurisdiction-specific conclusion.

If any operationally important item is unclear or missing, pause the assessment and seek current clarification from an authoritative Ronin source.
