## Where tokenized securities fit—and where they do not

A tokenized security is not interchangeable with every crypto asset or with the technology used to record or transfer an asset. The evidence here describes financial tokenization broadly as financial assets and liabilities represented on programmable digital ledgers; that description does not establish that any particular asset is a security or that a particular firm participates in tokenization. ([source](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/imf-notes/issues/2026/04/01/tokenized-finance-574921>))

The distinction matters when interpreting a company reference. Discussion of tokenized securities, digital-asset market structure, or a technology’s possible role would not alone prove adoption, a product launch, or an operating arrangement. Even a token record does not itself settle legal title, enforceability, or the quality of the represented asset.

For issuer-sponsored tokenized securities, the SEC staff statement describes possible approaches in which a crypto network forms part of the master ownership record or a token triggers updates to an authoritative offchain record. Which record is legally effective depends on governing documents, applicable law, and transfer mechanics. This describes possible structures, not a Citadel Securities implementation or a general regulatory determination. ([source](<https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities>))

## How to evaluate new claims

When a new claim appears, check the record before repeating it:

- Confirm that the source names Citadel Securities itself and identifies the relevant legal entity.
- Record the date, speaker or filing organization, and exact language. Distinguish a direct company statement from a third-party report or a policy proposal.
- Separate what the source directly establishes from what someone infers. A discussion of technology is not proof of a product, customer relationship, or deployment.
- For claims about legal effect, custody, availability, fees, security, recovery, or compliance, require evidence that addresses that specific subject and relevant jurisdiction.
- Check for later documentation before presenting a time-sensitive statement as current.
