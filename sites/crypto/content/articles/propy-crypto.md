Next, match each statement to the evidence needed to support it. “Crypto-related” is not the same as “has a token.” “Has a token” is not the same as “offers card payments.” A claim about card spending needs direct support for the spending function; a claim about payment processing, conversion, or settlement needs evidence for those specific steps. Keep project descriptions, marketing language, third-party commentary, and documented functionality distinct.
