## What AMP crypto is

AMP is a crypto token associated with collateral mechanisms for payment applications, according to the topic brief. In this context, **collateral** means an asset designated to support an obligation or reduce exposure while a payment is being processed. That general meaning does not, by itself, specify who controls the collateral, who can claim it, or what happens if a transaction fails.

## How AMP’s collateral role is designed to work
