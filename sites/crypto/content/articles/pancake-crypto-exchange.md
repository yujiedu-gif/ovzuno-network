## How a decentralized exchange processes a trade

In a pool-based DEX model, a smart contract provides trading logic, while a liquidity pool holds assets available for swaps. A blockchain is where a submitted transaction can be included in a block and settle. These roles are conceptually distinct: the contract applies its programmed rules, the pool supplies trading inventory, and the blockchain records the resulting transaction when included. The available evidence supports this general model, not a platform-specific transaction path. [source](<https://www.bis.org/publ/work1227.pdf>)

An automated market maker, or AMM, is a trading design in which pool-based mechanisms determine swap execution rather than relying only on a conventional order book. “Pool liquidity” means assets made available in a pool for trades.
