## What a Candy WWE NFT refers to

An NFT, or non-fungible token, is a token record whose creation and initial ownership follow a smart contract’s rules. Minting creates that token record and assigns initial ownership according to those rules; it does not, by itself, create, authenticate, store, or transfer rights in the work or asset the token may reference. For Ethereum NFTs, a contract can record ownership by associating a unique token identifier with an address. ## Token ownership versus rights to WWE-related content

A blockchain record of token ownership is not the same as ownership of copyright or the associated work. Transferring an NFT does not necessarily transfer rights in its associated physical or digital asset, or copyright rights in the underlying work. Those rights depend on applicable law and valid contracts, licenses, or other governing terms. ([source](<https://www.copyright.gov/policy/nft-study/Joint-USPTO-USCO-Report-on-NFTs-and-Intellectual-Property.pdf>))

NFT technology may support workflows for managing, transferring, or licensing intellectual-property rights when valid legal terms and authorized rights holders are present. But that general capability does not show that a specific Candy WWE NFT includes a license or any particular permission. The applicable terms would need to state what a holder may do.
