XLM is the crypto asset associated with the Stellar network. That identifies the asset’s network relationship, but it does not, by itself, establish what every Stellar transaction requires or which functions XLM performs. Institutional readers should verify those technical details in current, authoritative Stellar documentation before relying on them.

## What crypto XLM means

XLM names the crypto asset associated with Stellar; Stellar names the network. The distinction is between an asset and the network with which it is associated. The evidence available for this article supports that relationship, but does not provide a current primary Stellar source defining XLM’s full technical role or confirming its official terminology in detail. Where precise naming matters, check Stellar’s current documentation.

The distinction also separates protocol behavior from the way a business or service uses an asset. A company, application, exchange, or other provider may describe a product in terms of XLM. That description does not establish that the network requires the same activity for all users or operations. To understand a particular claim, identify whether it concerns a network rule, an application feature, or a provider’s own service.

## How XLM differs from a company share or account balance
