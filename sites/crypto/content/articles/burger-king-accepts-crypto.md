## What does “accepts crypto” mean for a restaurant purchase?

For this question, **direct acceptance** means Burger King itself—or an authorized ordering channel acting for the restaurant—offers cryptocurrency as a payment method for the order. The key issue is not simply whether crypto appears somewhere in a payment chain. It is whether the restaurant’s own payment process identifies crypto as an accepted method for that specific purchase.

A customer might use a card or another service connected to cryptocurrency. That does not, by itself, show that the restaurant accepts cryptocurrency directly: the restaurant may receive payment through a different method. Similarly, a third-party service could be involved in a payment arrangement without establishing that every Burger King location, or even the location in question, offers crypto as a standard option.

The ordering channel also matters. In-store payment, an official app, and a website checkout are separate places to verify. Evidence about one channel does not establish that the same method is available in the others. A useful answer is therefore specific: it names the location or order, the channel, and whether the option is presented as direct cryptocurrency payment.

## How to verify before placing an order

Use a short, purchase-specific check:

1. **Choose the location and channel.** Select the restaurant you intend to use and open its official app or website checkout, if ordering online.
2. **Read the payment options shown for that order.** Look for an explicit cryptocurrency option rather than inferring acceptance from a card or other service that may be associated with crypto.
3. **Ask in person when applicable.** Ask whether the location accepts cryptocurrency directly and what payment method it supports. If the answer refers to an outside provider, clarify whether that provider is part of the restaurant’s payment process or a separate service you would use.
4. **Confirm the terms before authorizing.** Check the amount due, the asset and network requested if crypto is offered, and any conversion information or fees displayed. Ask how refunds or payment errors are handled before proceeding if those terms are unclear.

Payment instructions are not interchangeable across all assets and networks. Before authorizing a crypto payment, compare the exact asset and network with the recipient’s instructions; compatibility and acceptance depend on the requested asset, network, destination, recipient requirements, and service. If the instructions or recipient cannot be confirmed, do not treat an apparent checkout option as sufficient verification.
