## How to assess a claimed infrastructure role

If a document or website claims a connection, assess the claim at the level of detail it actually supports:

- **Entity:** Does it name Fuse Crypto Limited exactly, or does it refer only to a brand, product, or similarly named organization?
- **Network and activity:** Does it identify the relevant network and say whether the entity operates a validator, supplies infrastructure, provides software, or performs another role?
- **Attribution:** Is the claim in first-party technical material or independently verifiable evidence, and can it be linked to the named legal entity rather than an unrelated operator?
- **Time and scope:** Is the information current, and does it describe a specific service or a broader claim? Do not extend a narrow disclosure to other networks or activities.

These checks help separate a documented relationship from an unverified assertion. They do not establish performance or safety. The available evidence does not substantiate claims about uptime, security controls, rewards, custody, or operating results for Fuse Crypto Limited.
