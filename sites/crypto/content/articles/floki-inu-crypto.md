## How to verify FLOKI information

Use a claim-by-claim process rather than treating one description as evidence for every related statement:

1. **Identify the asset.** Seek current, attributable documentation that names the network and exact contract address. The evidence available here does not verify those identifiers.
2. **Match technical evidence to the assertion.** A supply or transfer statement should be supported by evidence for the identified token and network—not merely by a project name or general description.
3. **Check product claims separately.** Find documentation for the named service or feature and see whether it explicitly describes how, if at all, FLOKI relates to access or use.
4. **Separate intent from operation.** A statement of intended function is not proof that a feature is implemented, working, or currently available. Seek evidence that addresses the specific claim and its timing.
5. **Keep rights and outcomes within scope.** Do not infer a legal right, security property, recovery path, fee, yield, or jurisdictional availability from a token label or ecosystem description.

This method is useful because token identity, contract behavior, service operation, and user rights are different questions. Evidence can support one without establishing the rest. If a source does not identify the token or service precisely, or does not address the relevant time and conditions, treat the claim as unverified rather than filling in missing details.
