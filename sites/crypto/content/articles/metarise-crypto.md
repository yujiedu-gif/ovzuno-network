## How to assess claims about MetaRise crypto

Start by treating each consequential statement as a separate claim. A statement about identity does not establish capabilities; a capability description does not establish current availability, security, regulatory status, or institutional use. Check whether the evidence supports the exact statement being made, rather than a broader conclusion inferred from related language.

For each claim, ask:

- **Who is responsible?** Look for an attributable organization and a clear connection between that organization and the named product, service, or protocol.
- **What is being described?** Determine whether the source concerns a business, software, a network, a token, or a service. Do not assume these are interchangeable.
- **What function is claimed?** Identify whether the claim concerns custody, transaction handling, accounting, governance, or something else. Avoid treating one function as proof of another.
- **What context applies?** Check for a product version, operating context, intended users, and any limits stated in the documentation.
- **What corroborates it?** For consequential claims, look for evidence that can be checked independently and that refers to the same entity and product.

A source’s origin and purpose matter. Attributable documentation can show what an organization says about a product, but a statement of intended capabilities is not automatically independent verification that those capabilities operate as described. Promotional descriptions, anonymous assertions, and repeated claims without traceable evidence should not be treated as confirmation.

Technical terms require the same care. A zero-knowledge rollup, for example, moves execution and state storage offchain while submitting state-change summaries and cryptographic validity proofs to Ethereum. Proof systems, data-publication policies, operator designs, and escape mechanisms vary by implementation. This is an example of a bounded technology description, not evidence that MetaRise uses a rollup or offers any treasury service. [source](<https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/scaling/zk-rollups/>)

Treat security, recovery, compliance, regulatory status, service availability, and service terms as unverified unless suitable, current evidence supports each claim. A technical description alone does not establish how an organization secures or recovers access, and the evidence supplied here does not identify MetaRise’s jurisdiction or provide documentation for these matters.
