## What a Bored Ape NFT is

An NFT, or non-fungible token, is a distinct token recorded under a blockchain token system. In the ERC-721 standard, an individual token is identified within its contract by the combination of the contract address and token ID on an Ethereum chain. This provides a way to identify the token; it does not, by itself, verify the identity of an associated image or establish what rights apply.

“Bored Ape NFT” describes a token associated with an item in the Bored Ape collection. The token is a blockchain record. Artwork is digital content, which can be a separate object from that record. This distinction matters because a token’s identification and the content people associate with it are not interchangeable: one names the token under its contract, while the other is the artwork or file being referenced.
