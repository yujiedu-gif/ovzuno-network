## What evidence would show a staking or validator role?

A staking claim needs documentation that explicitly connects the identified VEMP asset to a staking function and explains what that function does. A validator claim needs evidence that connects the same asset and network to validator participation. A general description of proof of stake, or of staking on another network, cannot establish either VEMP-specific claim.

For comparison only, Ethereum’s proof-of-stake design uses ETH deposits as slashable economic collateral for validator participation. ## How to verify claims without conflating VEMP with proof of stake

To assess a VEMP staking or validator claim, use a short evidence check:

1. **Resolve the identity.** Find attributable project documentation that identifies the asset, its network, and—where provided—the token contract or other network identifier. Confirm that the source refers to the same VEMP named in the claim.
2. **Read the stated function.** Look for documentation that directly explains whether staking exists and what it accomplishes. Do not infer staking from a token name or from descriptions of proof of stake in general.
3. **Check validator wording separately.** Determine whether the material says VEMP itself is used in validator participation, rather than merely mentioning validators, delegates, or network security in general.
4. **Compare against network evidence.** Where available, consult records or technical documentation for the same network and token. Distinguish a project’s description of intended use from evidence of behavior on the network.
5. **Track context.** Make sure the evidence concerns the same asset, network, and documentation version as the claim. If those details are missing or do not match, treat the claim as unresolved.

Until those materials are available and aligned, the careful conclusion is not that VEMP definitely has or lacks a staking role. It is that the evidence considered here cannot verify one. General proof-of-stake references can clarify terminology, but they cannot fill that VEMP-specific evidence gap.
