## How to check the website or provider behind the phrase

Begin with the exact web address. Compare the spelling and domain ending with any address given in company materials. Do not assume that a page using a familiar-looking name is operated by the organization it resembles. The address is a starting point for identification, not a substitute for evidence about the operator.

Next, look for the legal entity named in the site’s terms, privacy information, and product documentation. Check whether those materials identify an organization responsible for the service, provide contact details, and use consistent names. If the page offers a card but does not say which entity operates the service, that is an unanswered verification question.

Then compare the site’s statements with current, independently attributable information connected to the named organization. A statement on the site can establish what the site claims, but a site’s own claim does not independently verify the underlying fact. Look for primary documentation that ties the domain, company, and described service together. If the names or descriptions do not align, pause and resolve the discrepancy before relying on the offer.
