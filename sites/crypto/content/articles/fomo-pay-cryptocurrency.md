## How to assess any claimed payment flow

If a source claims that a company handles a cryptocurrency payment, trace the roles rather than relying on a broad label such as “crypto payments.” Identify who initiates the payment, who receives it, whether an intermediary converts the asset, and which parties handle settlement. Also note whether the claim describes the company itself or another provider involved in the flow. These distinctions help define the claim being checked; they do not establish that FOMO Pay performs any of these functions.

Next, verify the exact asset and network, the settlement currency, and the event the service treats as completion. A payment description that names a cryptocurrency does not necessarily establish support for every version of that asset or every network. For example, a stablecoin may exist on multiple blockchains while relying on a shared off-chain reserve and issuer-operated issuance and redemption system. The token, contract, bridge representation, and redemption support may nevertheless differ by chain.
