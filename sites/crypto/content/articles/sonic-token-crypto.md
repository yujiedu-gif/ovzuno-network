## What does Sonic token crypto refer to?

A token and a blockchain are different kinds of things. A blockchain is a network with rules for recording and validating activity; a token is an asset or record whose behavior depends on the relevant network and its implementation. Calling something a “Sonic token” suggests an association, but the phrase alone does not verify which asset is meant, whether it is native to a network or issued through a contract, or whether an asset with that name is current.

To make the identification precise, documentation would need to connect the asset to the intended network.
