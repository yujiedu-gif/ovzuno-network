## What the documentation does not establish

Even if a project document is available, its statements should not be expanded beyond their scope. Documentation can explain a stated design; it does not automatically establish that an implementation performs as intended, that a service remains available, or that a user will receive a particular result. Those conclusions need evidence specific to the relevant contract, network, application, or provider. No such XEN-specific evidence is supplied here.

The same discipline applies to fees, yields, recovery procedures, and compliance claims. These are not established by a token’s name or by a general description of a protocol. A fee claim requires current information for the relevant action and network. A yield claim requires evidence identifying its source and conditions. A recovery claim requires documentation for the wallet, service, or other mechanism involved. Compliance claims require current evidence specific to the arrangement and relevant jurisdiction. The permitted evidence for this article does not substantiate any of those XEN-specific points.
