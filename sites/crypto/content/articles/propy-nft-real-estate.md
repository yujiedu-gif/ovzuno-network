## What “Propy NFT real estate” refers to

The phrase describes a topic involving NFTs and real estate transactions, but it does not identify one uniform transaction structure. An NFT is a blockchain token; in a real estate context, it may be discussed alongside transaction information or a legal arrangement. Those connections must be verified for the specific offering rather than assumed from the token’s existence.

An Ethereum NFT contract can record ownership by associating a unique token identifier with an Ethereum address. That is a record of token ownership under the contract’s rules. It does not, on its own, show that the address holder is recognized as the owner of a particular parcel of land. Nor does a token’s name or description establish what rights it represents.

A useful distinction is between three things: the token entry, documents or records linked to the transaction, and the legal rights in the property.
