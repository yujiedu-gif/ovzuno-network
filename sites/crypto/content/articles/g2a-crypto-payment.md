## How to verify a claimed payment option

Begin with the merchant’s official checkout, reached through a channel you have independently verified. Then consult current official help or support materials if the listed payment choices or instructions are unclear. Check the country and transaction context that apply to your situation; a general reference to a payment method does not prove that it applies to every customer or checkout.

Read the complete instruction before taking action. Confirm that it identifies the intended asset and network, and compare the destination and requested payment flow with the details in official information. If the request and checkout do not match, pause. Do not guess which asset, network, or destination was intended. Compatibility depends on the request, destination, recipient requirements, and service, so the discrepancy needs clarification from a verified official source. Be especially careful with unsolicited messages, external links, or unexpected prompts asking you to send funds. Independently compare its instructions with official information rather than using the message to replace or modify details shown at checkout.

If official material does not answer an important question—for example, whether the named asset and network apply to this transaction—do not infer an answer from the keyword or from a similar-looking instruction. Seek clarification through a verified official channel before proceeding. This is a verification approach, not a claim that a particular support team offers a specific process or outcome.
