## How to verify claims and project status

Readers assessing future claims can use a short evidence trail. First, identify the publisher and the exact entity described. Record when the material was published and whether it identifies a project, token, protocol, or application. If it gives a network, address, or other technical identifier, preserve that detail rather than relying on a name or symbol alone. Then compare claims across attributable materials and check whether they refer to the same entity.

For claims about implementation, look for records that can be examined independently and determine whether they correspond to the identifiers in the project materials. An explorer entry may help investigate an onchain claim, but the existence of an entry does not independently establish the project’s purpose, the entry’s connection to a named project, or the project’s overall operating status.
