## What Fantom staking involves

Staking generally means committing cryptocurrency as stake, with that stake influencing consensus participation or weight. This is a broad description, not a complete set of Fantom instructions: selection, delegation, custody, withdrawal, rewards, and penalties are protocol-specific.

A validator performs duties defined by its network. Those duties can include participating in consensus, but the precise tasks and requirements depend on the protocol. A delegator, by contrast, assigns stake to support a validator where that network offers delegation. Delegating does not, by itself, establish that the delegator operates the validator’s infrastructure or performs its protocol duties.

One network’s delegation model cannot be treated as Fantom’s. For example, Solana documentation describes a model in which a delegator assigns stake to a validator, increasing its consensus weight while the delegator retains token control under the stake-account model. That is a Solana-specific example, not evidence of Fantom’s controls or mechanics.
