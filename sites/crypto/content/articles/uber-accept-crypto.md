## How to check Uber payment options

Start with the payment methods displayed in your Uber app for the intended trip. Check whether the specific method you plan to use is listed and selectable before relying on it. If the choice is missing, disabled, or unclear, consult current official Uber information rather than inferring availability from a general statement, an old description, or a third party’s capabilities.

Use a focused verification sequence:

1. Open the payment options relevant to the trip you plan to take.
2. Identify the exact method you intend to use; do not substitute a similarly named wallet or an outside service without checking what the app actually offers.
3. Confirm that the method is selectable for your account and trip before depending on it.
4. If you are considering a crypto-funded card or conversion service, verify that route separately with the provider and do not label it direct Uber crypto acceptance.

This check answers a practical, limited question: what does the app display and allow for this account and trip? It does not establish a universal payment list for all riders or locations. Nor does an option displayed in the app prove anything about a separate provider’s conversion process or terms.

Treat the app and official Uber information as the sources to check for Uber’s side of the payment choice. Treat an outside provider’s own current documentation as the source to check for its crypto service. Neither source should be used to make claims about the other party’s terms unless it explicitly supports them.
