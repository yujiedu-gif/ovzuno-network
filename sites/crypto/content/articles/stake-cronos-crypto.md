## How staking participation and validators fit together

Staking arrangements vary by network. As examples of why network-specific documentation matters, Solana documentation describes a delegator assigning stake to a validator, increasing that validator’s consensus weight while the delegator retains token control under Solana’s stake-account model. Tezos documentation, by contrast, describes staked funds as being at risk of protocol slashing for specified delegate misbehavior, unlike unstaked delegation balances. These are examples of other networks—not evidence of Cronos behavior.

For Cronos, verify whether participants delegate assets to validators, operate a validator themselves, or use another documented route. If delegation is supported, confirm what authority the participant retains and what the validator is responsible for.
