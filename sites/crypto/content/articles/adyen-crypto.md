## What does “Adyen crypto” refer to?

The phrase can refer to different things, so begin by naming the capability the business actually needs. Direct cryptocurrency acceptance means a customer pays with cryptocurrency. That is distinct from accepting a different payment method, receiving fiat settlement, or using a service that involves digital assets somewhere in the payment chain. Confirmation of one capability does not establish the others.

For example, a product description might concern payment acceptance, settlement, or a service provided by another party. Those descriptions answer different questions about who handles the customer’s payment, who may hold or transfer funds, and what the merchant receives. Do not infer that a provider accepts crypto directly, converts it, holds it, or settles proceeds in a particular currency unless current product materials and applicable terms explicitly establish that arrangement.

The first verification task is therefore not to interpret the phrase in a search result. It is to find a current, official description of the merchant-facing product and match it to the business’s intended customer-to-merchant flow. If the description does not clearly identify that flow, treat the capability as unresolved rather than reading a general payment statement as proof of cryptocurrency support.

## How to verify Adyen's current crypto-payment offering

Start with current official Adyen documentation and merchant-facing terms. Look for explicit language describing cryptocurrency acceptance, not only broad references to payments, technology, or digital assets. Identify the product name, the parties involved, and what happens between a customer initiating payment and the merchant receiving and reconciling funds.

Then trace the proposed flow step by step. Determine whether the customer transfers cryptocurrency, whether any party converts it, and what asset or currency the merchant receives. If a third-party provider is named, check the applicable terms to establish that party’s role and the responsibilities of each participant. Do not assume that a partner reference or general product description answers questions about custody, payment status, refunds, or settlement.

For the specific product and flow under consideration, seek explicit answers to these questions:

- Can the customer pay with cryptocurrency directly, or does the product involve another payment method?
- Which assets and networks are supported, if any? Are only specified token representations accepted?
- What does the merchant receive, and which party performs any conversion?
- How are payment status, exceptions, refunds, disputes, and reconciliation addressed in the product terms?
- Which entities contract with the merchant or otherwise take part in the transaction?

A missing or ambiguous answer should remain an open item in the evaluation. It does not prove either that a service exists or that it does not. In particular, the available evidence does not establish Adyen’s supported crypto assets, networks, product flow, or settlement arrangements.
