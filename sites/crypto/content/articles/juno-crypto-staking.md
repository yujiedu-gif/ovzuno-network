## What participation in Juno staking involves

Staking rules are network-specific. The available evidence does not identify the asset used for Juno staking or confirm whether Juno currently supports both delegation and direct validator operation. It therefore cannot establish a reliable participation flow, required steps, or current terminology for the Juno network.

This distinction matters because a general description of staking cannot confirm how a particular network assigns consensus weight, handles delegated assets, or authorizes validator activity. For example, Solana documentation describes a model in which a delegator assigns stake to a validator, increasing that validator’s consensus weight while the delegator retains token control under the stake-account model. That is a Solana-specific description and should not be treated as evidence of Juno behavior. [source](<https://solana.com/docs/references/staking>)

No allowed evidence establishes Juno’s reward calculation, reward distribution schedule, unbonding period, access restrictions, redelegation rules, or slashing conditions. As a result, no rate, expected return, withdrawal timeline, or penalty outcome can be stated here.
