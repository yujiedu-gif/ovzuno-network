## What the BlockFi crypto credit card was

The central distinction is between using credit to make a card purchase and spending cryptocurrency to fund one. In a credit-card arrangement, the purchase is made under a credit agreement, and the cardholder’s repayment obligations are governed by that agreement. A crypto-funded payment would instead involve a mechanism that draws on crypto assets or converts them to fund the purchase. The available evidence does not establish that BlockFi’s card used the latter mechanism. Its crypto-reward association alone does not show that a cardholder’s own crypto was sold or transferred at checkout.

The phrase “crypto credit card” is not, by itself, a complete description of the product’s contractual structure.
