## What “The DAO” meant in Ethereum’s history

“The DAO” refers here to one particular Ethereum-associated project. The definite article matters: it identifies a named historical project rather than describing every organization that uses the DAO label. This article concerns that project and the incident associated with it, not how to join, create, or govern a DAO today.

It is also useful to keep the project distinct from Ethereum itself. Ethereum’s protocol is the network’s shared technical rules and operation; a project built on Ethereum is a separate system that may rely on those rules. The evidence available here does not establish the project’s complete purpose, organizational structure, or specific contractual arrangements, so a more detailed definition would require project-specific records.
