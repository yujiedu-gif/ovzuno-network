## What RMRK crypto refers to

In ordinary technical usage, a protocol is a set of rules or interfaces that software follows. An asset is something represented or handled under a system’s rules. These categories can be related, but they are not synonyms. A protocol may provide tools for creating or managing assets, while an asset may have a separate identifier and properties. The existence of a protocol does not, by itself, establish that it has a tradable token or specify what such a token would do.
