## How a wallet differs from a blockchain

A wallet-related term and a blockchain name point to different kinds of things. A wallet is generally a software or service layer used in connection with crypto assets and networks. A blockchain, by contrast, is a distributed ledger maintained according to a protocol. The general definition of blockchain describes a shared ledger, not an application that a person might use to interact with it.

The distinction does not establish that any particular product supports any particular network. Nor does it mean that a wallet creates, owns, or operates every network with which it may interact. Product identity, integrations, and network operation are separate facts, and each needs evidence about the relevant product or protocol.
