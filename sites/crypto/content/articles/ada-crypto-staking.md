## What ADA crypto staking means

Proof of stake is a consensus model in which cryptocurrency committed as stake influences a participant’s consensus weight. This is a general definition, not a description of Cardano’s particular protocol or of the actions an ADA holder takes when delegating. [source](<https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/proof_of_stake_consensus_model>)

The available evidence does not support a Cardano-specific account of whether delegation requires transferring ADA, how a delegation choice is recorded, whether the holder retains control, or how stake pools participate. Those details matter: “delegation” should not be assumed to mean the same thing across different networks or services. A general description of Solana, for example, says its delegators can assign stake to validators while retaining token control under Solana’s stake-account model; that is specifically about Solana and cannot establish ADA behavior. [source](<https://solana.com/docs/references/staking>)

Likewise, Ethereum’s proof-of-stake model uses ETH deposits as slashable collateral for validator participation. That Ethereum-specific arrangement does not show that ADA holders must deposit funds as collateral, run validator infrastructure, or face the same protocol consequences. [source](<https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/>)
