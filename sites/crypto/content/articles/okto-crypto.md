## How to verify the product or organization behind the name

Begin with a current official source attributable to the organization. Look for an explicit product description, the legal entity providing the service, and documentation that applies to the specific product and operating entity. A brand name or search-result snippet does not establish those details.

Next, compare the documented function with the treasury use case under consideration. A source might describe a wallet, payment service, onramp, custody arrangement, or another function. Do not infer a broader treasury role from the label alone. As one example of a bounded description, an onramp quote API can return estimated conversion outcomes based on inputs such as source amount, destination cryptocurrency, network, fees, and rate before a purchase flow. That example illustrates a limited function; it is not evidence about Okto or proof that an onramp is treasury infrastructure. ([source](<https://docs.stripe.com/api/crypto/onramp_quotes>))

Check whether the source is current and whether its stated scope matches the product being researched. A description for one service or entity should not be treated as evidence about another product with a similar name. If official material does not identify the entity, connect it to the product, or explain the function, leave those points unresolved rather than filling gaps from branding or unrelated examples.
