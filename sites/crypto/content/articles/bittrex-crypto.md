## How an exchange fits into crypto market infrastructure

A crypto exchange is a market-facing service, while a blockchain is a separate system that maintains transaction records. A general centralized-exchange model can provide near-real-time trading, liquid order books, and advanced order functionality after assets are deposited with the venue. These capabilities describe a possible general model; they do not establish that Bittrex offered them, that a particular venue offers them today, or that performance and liquidity are the same across venues, asset pairs, and market conditions. When examining an exchange-related statement, separate the activity of the trading venue from the custody arrangement and from any blockchain record. A statement that a transaction appears on a blockchain does not, on its own, establish what an exchange offers or whether a customer can use a service. Likewise, evidence about general trading functionality does not establish how a named company handled custody or settlement. Each claim needs evidence relevant to its specific subject.

More importantly, an on-chain lookup is not a substitute for evidence about a company’s current services, operating status, or customer arrangements. [^2]
