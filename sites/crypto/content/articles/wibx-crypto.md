Those are separate questions. A project name does not, by itself, establish which blockchain records a token. A network reference does not establish that a particular contract is the project’s token. And a contract record does not alone confirm that the project’s description of its purpose is accurate. Reliable identification needs a traceable connection among the claimed project, the technical identifier, and the relevant network records.
