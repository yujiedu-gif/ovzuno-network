## What “the DAO token” refers to

“The DAO token” points to an asset associated with one particular project, The DAO. The project name, its token, and the broader idea of a decentralized autonomous organization are separate things. A label that links an asset to a project does not, by itself, explain the asset’s technical functions or establish a holder’s rights.

The distinction matters because DAO governance is not one fixed mechanism. It can involve proposals, voting, delegation, token-based membership, share-based membership, reputation, or multisignature execution. These are examples of governance approaches, not a description of The DAO’s specific rules. Evidence about a general category cannot establish what one historical project implemented.
