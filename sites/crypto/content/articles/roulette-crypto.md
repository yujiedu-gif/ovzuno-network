## What “roulette crypto” means

The phrase brings together two separate elements: roulette as a game, and cryptocurrency as a possible means of transferring value. A cryptocurrency system can involve protocol rules, a transaction ledger, and network participants that update, store, and read the ledger. That payment infrastructure is distinct from the rules governing a roulette game. [source](<https://www.bis.org/publications/aer-2018/cryptocurrencies-looking-beyond-hype>)
