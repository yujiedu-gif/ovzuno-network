## How to distinguish a product, project, and digital asset

Start by asking what the source actually describes. A device or service is not necessarily a blockchain project; a project is not necessarily a digital asset; and evidence that an asset exists would not alone identify its issuer or operator. A careful account should separately establish the category, the company’s stated role, the source’s date, and whether another attributable source corroborates a material claim.

Ownership and responsibility also depend on the particular arrangement. In a self-custody model, the user controls and is responsible for the private keys and recovery material needed to access the user’s crypto assets. That general description does not establish that Sirin Labs offered a self-custody product, used a particular recovery design, or had responsibility for an asset. Tokenized arrangements can also differ in what the token record represents. Issuer-sponsored models may use a crypto network as part of the master ownership record, or use onchain transfers to trigger updates to an authoritative offchain record. This describes possible models, not evidence that Sirin Labs issued a token or used either arrangement. ([source](<https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities>))

NFT technology may support intellectual-property management, transfer, or licensing workflows when valid legal terms and authorized rights holders are present.
