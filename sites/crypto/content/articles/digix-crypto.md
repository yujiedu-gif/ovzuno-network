## What technical details should readers verify?

Use a stepwise check rather than relying on a name alone:

1. **Identify the entity.** Find a primary document naming the Digix project, protocol, token, or service being discussed. Note the document’s date and what it actually covers.
2. **Identify the network and asset.** For a token claim, look for the exact official name, network, and contract address. Confirm that the technical record refers to the same asset described in the project documentation.
3. **Match the claim to the evidence.** A source about a project does not necessarily establish token mechanics; a source about a token does not necessarily establish custody, backing, or service status.
