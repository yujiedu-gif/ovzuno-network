“Crypto on Amazon” can refer to different arrangements. The phrase alone does not tell you who accepts the payment or how it reaches an order.

Direct cryptocurrency checkout would mean that the marketplace itself offers cryptocurrency as a payment option in its checkout flow. An indirect route involves another party or a separate product that may be connected to a purchase.
