## What “mining” means in the Helium network

The word *mining* does not, by itself, identify the consensus or participation mechanism a network uses. In Bitcoin’s proof-of-work process, software constructs a candidate block and header, and mining hardware searches by varying a nonce until the resulting hash satisfies the network’s target condition. That is a Bitcoin-specific mechanism. It is not evidence that Helium discovers blocks through hashing or uses the same rules. [1]
