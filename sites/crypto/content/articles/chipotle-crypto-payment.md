## What “Chipotle crypto payment” means

Direct cryptocurrency acceptance means the merchant presents cryptocurrency as a way to pay for an order. An intermediary route is different: a separate service might offer a gift card, prepaid balance, or another product that can be used toward an order. Using such a service would not, by itself, show that Chipotle accepts cryptocurrency directly or endorses the service.

To distinguish the routes, trace the payment path. Ask who receives the cryptocurrency, what the customer receives in return, and which party processes or fulfills the restaurant order. A cryptocurrency transfer can be authorized by a digital signature under the relevant network’s rules, but that technical authorization does not establish the identity of the person or organization controlling the receiving key, nor does it show that Chipotle accepts the transfer.
