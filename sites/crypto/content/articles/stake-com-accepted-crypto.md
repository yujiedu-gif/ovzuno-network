## What “accepted” means for a deposit

Here, “accepted” means that current official instructions identify an asset as available for deposit through a specified route. It is a narrow statement about depositing, not a general endorsement or a guarantee that every account can use the option.

Deposit support does not, by itself, establish that withdrawals are supported or that the asset can be used in a staking, delegation, validator, or yield product. These are separate service claims and require separate, current product evidence. For example, Ethereum proof-of-stake uses ETH deposits as slashable economic collateral for validator participation; that protocol fact does not establish that a platform offers validator services or accepts ETH deposits for its customers.
