## What does Polygon mean in crypto?

The word “Polygon” may be used to refer to a project, its blockchain infrastructure, or the applications and assets connected with its ecosystem. Those meanings overlap in ordinary discussion, but they answer different questions. “Which network?” asks about the technical environment and its rules. “Which token?” asks about a crypto asset recorded and used under particular rules. “Which application?” asks about software built to interact with a network or asset.

These distinctions help avoid a common category error: treating the token as if it were the network, or treating the network as if it were a single application. A network can provide infrastructure for software without being the software itself. An asset may be recorded on a network without being identical to the network or every other asset in its ecosystem.

Even a project association does not establish that all related assets share an issuer, purpose, technical role, or rights. The name alone cannot identify which chain a transaction uses, whether a token is native to that chain, or what its protocol function is. Those questions require evidence about the specific network and asset, not a general project description.
