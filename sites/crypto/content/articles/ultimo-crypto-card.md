## How to verify a claimed card product

Begin with the source making the claim. Look for official product terms or operational documentation published by an identifiable provider. Check that the materials state the exact product name and identify the entity responsible for it. Then look for a clear statement of the issuer and, if claimed, the card network. A name appearing in a copied description or an unattributed listing does not independently resolve those questions.

Next, check whether the documentation explains the transaction flow. Does it say how a card is funded, whether conversion occurs, when it occurs, how a payment is authorized, and how settlement is handled? These are separate operational questions. A claim about one does not establish the others, and the phrase “crypto card” is not a substitute for product-specific documentation.

Use a simple evidence record while reviewing materials: write down the claim, the document that supports it, the document’s date or version if shown, and what the document leaves unanswered. This helps distinguish a provider’s stated terms from third-party claims and assumptions. If a document does not answer a question, record it as unknown rather than filling the gap from descriptions of other cards.
