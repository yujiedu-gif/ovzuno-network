---
title: Editorial Standards
description: Editorial standards for OvZuno Crypto.
kicker: Publishing principles
---

Claims should be traceable to protocol documentation, regulatory material, transaction or market infrastructure data, security disclosures, or clearly identified expert evidence.

Articles distinguish technical explanation from risk interpretation. We do not invent audits, certifications, reserves, security findings, named reviewers, or investment outcomes. Material corrections receive a `lastmod` value only after a substantive verified change.

These rules operate under the [OvZuno network standards](https://ovzuno.com/editorial-standards/).
