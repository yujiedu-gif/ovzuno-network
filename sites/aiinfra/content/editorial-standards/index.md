title: Editorial Standards
description: Editorial standards for OvZuno AI Infrastructure.
kicker: Publishing principles
---

Technical claims should be traceable to primary documentation, standards, direct data, or clearly identified expert evidence. Articles distinguish documented behavior from analysis and state the operating assumptions behind performance or cost conclusions.

We do not invent benchmarks, deployments, customer experience, named reviewers, or engineering credentials. Material corrections are made directly and receive a `lastmod` value only after a substantive verified change.

These publication-specific rules operate under the [OvZuno network standards](https://ovzuno.com/editorial-standards/).
