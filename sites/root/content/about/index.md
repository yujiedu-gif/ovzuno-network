OvZuno is a B2B technology publisher built around a simple idea: complicated markets deserve publications with clear boundaries.

We create focused technology publications for builders, operators, and decision-makers. Our initial fields are AI infrastructure, crypto infrastructure and payments, and workplace technology.

## What OvZuno is

OvZuno is a publishing network. It does not present itself as a software platform, consulting company, product marketplace, analyst firm, or lead-generation service.

Each publication defines its audience and scope before it begins publishing. That discipline helps readers understand what a site can reliably cover—and what sits outside its remit.

## Independent knowledge, visible relationships

Commercial partners may supply source material or support a clearly labelled project. They do not receive control over editorial conclusions. Material relationships are disclosed where readers encounter the relevant content.

Our operating standards are described in [Editorial Standards](/editorial-standards/) and [Publication Disclosure](/publication-disclosure/).
