OvZuno may work with companies whose products or markets overlap with our coverage. Readers should be able to see those relationships without decoding vague labels.

## Partner-supported work

When a company funds, commissions, or materially supports a piece of content, the page will carry a clear disclosure such as **Partner-supported** and identify the supporting organization.

## Editorial control

Partners may provide factual source material, access, or subject suggestions. OvZuno retains control over structure, conclusions, limitations, corrections, and publication decisions.

## Links and mentions

A link or product mention does not by itself indicate endorsement or payment. Relevant commercial relationships will be disclosed separately.

Questions about a disclosure can be sent to [editor@ovzuno.com](mailto:editor@ovzuno.com).
