OvZuno publications are organized around a specific professional audience, a bounded field of coverage, and an editorial plan that can stand independently of any one company.

## AI Infrastructure

[OvZuno AI Infrastructure](https://aiinfra.ovzuno.com/) explains the systems behind training, inference, GPU cloud, model APIs, deployment, cost, and observability.

## Crypto

[OvZuno Crypto](https://crypto.ovzuno.com/) covers crypto infrastructure, stablecoins, payments, custody, security, and compliance technology without investment promotion.

## Workplace

[OvZuno Workplace](https://workplace.ovzuno.com/) covers unified communications, collaboration, contact centers, workplace agents, and workflow automation.

## How the network grows

A new publication is added only when it has a distinct audience, at least three viable topic clusters, and enough independent editorial value to exist without a client brand.
