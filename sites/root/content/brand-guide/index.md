## The mark

The OvZuno mark combines two open signal arcs, a restrained diagonal Z path, and one signal node. It is designed to suggest a clear signal without reproducing a target, orbit diagram, blockchain node, or financial symbol.

Use the complete lowercase wordmark wherever space permits. The symbol may be used alone for favicons and compact interface contexts.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least half the height of the circular mark. Do not place it inside another shape, add effects, redraw the arcs, or separate `zuno` from the rest of the word.

## Color

- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Cool surface: `#F4F7FA`
- Ink: `#122033`
- Cobalt: `#2D5BFF`
- Muted: `#647083`
- Rule: `#D8DCE3`

AI Infrastructure uses blue-cyan, Crypto uses copper-orange, and Workplace uses teal only as supporting accents. The master wordmark remains consistent.

Warm cream, beige, and large warm-gray surfaces are not part of the interface palette. Publication pages use white, cool gray, and ink blue so technical diagrams and editorial content retain a clean, neutral canvas.
