---
title: Editorial Standards
description: Editorial standards for OvZuno Workplace.
kicker: Publishing principles
---

Claims should be traceable to product documentation, standards, regulatory material, direct operating data, or clearly identified expert evidence. Articles distinguish available capabilities from implementation assumptions and marketing claims.

We do not invent deployments, customer outcomes, integrations, certifications, named reviewers, or productivity measurements. Material corrections receive a `lastmod` value only after a substantive verified change.

These rules operate under the [OvZuno network standards](https://ovzuno.com/editorial-standards/).
