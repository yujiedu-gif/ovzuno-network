OvZuno Workplace is for the people selecting, operating, integrating, and governing technology for communication and coordinated work.

## What we cover

Our coverage includes unified communications, collaboration platforms, contact centers, workplace agents, workflow automation, administration, interoperability, and operating measurement.

## What we leave out

We do not cover generic career advice, workplace lifestyle, broad human-resources commentary, or office furniture.

This publication is part of [OvZuno](https://ovzuno.com/), a B2B technology publisher. Its first editorial collection is in development and the site is not yet intended for search indexing.
